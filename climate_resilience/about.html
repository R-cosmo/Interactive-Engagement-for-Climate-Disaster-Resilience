About Climate Engage AU

Who We Are and What We Stand For

Our Mission

Climate Engage AU is a community-driven platform dedicated to building resilience against climate change and natural disasters across Australia. We connect individuals, communities, and organisations to share knowledge, resources, and action plans that make a real difference.

What We Do

Community Events

We organise local and national events to raise awareness and foster collaboration around climate resilience and disaster preparedness.

Educational Resources

We provide up-to-date resources, guides, and tools to help communities understand and respond to climate-related challenges.

Volunteer Network

Our contributors and volunteers work across Australia to support affected regions and drive grassroots climate action.

Our Team

We are a passionate group of climate advocates, researchers, developers, and community organisers united by a shared commitment to a safer, more resilient Australia.

Get Involved

Want to make a difference? Join our community and help build a more resilient future for all Australians.
